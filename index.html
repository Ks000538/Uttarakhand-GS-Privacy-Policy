Welcome to Uttarakhand GS

Uttarakhand GS is an independent educational application developed to help students, competitive examination aspirants and general learners prepare for examinations related to Uttarakhand General Knowledge.

We respect your privacy. This page explains how the application handles information, stores study progress and provides educational content.

Application: Uttarakhand GS
Version: 1.0 (Build 1)
Package ID: com.aistudio.ukgs.app

01 No Personal Data Collection

Uttarakhand GS is designed as a fully offline educational application.

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through the application.

The application does not request or collect:

  • Name or email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Contacts or personal photographs.
  • Personal device identifiers.
  • Account credentials or login information.

No registration, sign-in or account creation is required to use the application.

उत्तराखंड GS एक पूर्णतः ऑफलाइन शैक्षिक एप्लिकेशन है। यह उपयोगकर्ता का नाम, ईमेल, मोबाइल नंबर, लोकेशन, संपर्क, फोटो या अन्य व्यक्तिगत जानकारी एकत्र या किसी बाहरी सर्वर पर साझा नहीं करता है।

02 Local On-Device Storage Only

Your study progress and application preferences are stored locally on your device.

This includes:

  • Practice progress.
  • Quiz attempts and results.
  • Accuracy analytics.
  • Bookmarked questions.
  • Theme selection and app preferences.
  • Quiz length and other study settings.

This information is stored locally using Android Room SQLite database and local preference files. It is not transmitted to our external servers.

आपका अभ्यास, क्विज परिणाम, बुकमार्क और ऐप की सेटिंग्स केवल आपके डिवाइस में स्थानीय रूप से सुरक्षित रहती हैं।

03 No Advertisements, Analytics or Tracking

Uttarakhand GS does not include third-party advertising networks, behavioural analytics SDKs or tracking technologies.

The application does not use advertising identifiers or third-party tracking services.

इस एप्लिकेशन में कोई थर्ड-पार्टी विज्ञापन नेटवर्क, व्यवहार संबंधी एनालिटिक्स SDK या ट्रैकिंग तकनीक उपयोग नहीं की जाती है।

04 Android Permissions

The application is designed to function without requesting sensitive runtime Android permissions.

It does not require access to:

  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Location.
  • Contacts.
  • Personal photographs.

No sensitive runtime permissions are requested for the application's educational functionality.

05 Data Control and Deletion

You have control over your locally stored study records.

You can delete your information by:

  • Using Settings → Reset Study History, where available in the application.
  • Clearing the application's storage through Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

Clearing application storage or uninstalling the application removes its locally stored study records.

आप Settings में जाकर Reset Study History विकल्प से अपना अभ्यास रिकॉर्ड हटा सकते हैं। ऐप का डेटा Android Settings से क्लियर करने या ऐप अनइंस्टॉल करने पर भी स्थानीय डेटा हट जाता है।

06 Children's Privacy

Uttarakhand GS is designed for competitive examination aspirants and general learners.

Because the application does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Parents or guardians may contact the developer if they have any privacy-related concerns.
