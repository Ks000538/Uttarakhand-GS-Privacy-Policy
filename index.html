3 Google AdMob Advertising Disclosure

To support ongoing maintenance and keep educational content accessible for students, Uttarakhand GS intends to display and integrates Google AdMob to serve limited, non-intrusive banner advertisements strictly outside active quizzes and mock tests.

When your device is connected to the internet and advertisements are active, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and its partners may automatically collect and process certain device signals to serve, personalize (where consented), cap frequency, prevent fraud, and measure ads:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and app set ID / device identifiers
  • Approximate (coarse) location inferred from IP address
  • App interactions & diagnostics (ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, and performance metrics)
  • Device specifications (OS version, device model, screen size, language)

When you use the app offline, no ad requests are sent. Where required by regional laws (such as EEA/UK/GDPR), Uttarakhand GS uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow so you can choose your ad consent preferences. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

ऐप को विद्यार्थियों के लिए सुलभ बनाए रखने हेतु Google AdMob के माध्यम से सीमित एवं गैर-दखलंदाज़ी बैनर विज्ञापन (केवल सक्रिय क्विज़ व मॉक टेस्ट के बाहर) दिखाए जाने का प्रावधान है। ऑनलाइन होने पर Google AdMob विज्ञापन दिखाने व मापने हेतु Advertising ID, अनुमानित स्थान (IP आधारित) और तकनीकी डायग्नोस्टिक्स का उपयोग कर सकता है। ऑफलाइन उपयोग में कोई विज्ञापन अनुरोध नहीं भेजा जाता।

4 App Permissions & Daily Study Reminders

Uttarakhand GS requests only minimal permissions required for core features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+): Optional permission used solely to deliver your daily 5:00 PM study practice reminder locally on your device. You can enable or disable it anytime in Settings.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used only to restore your 5:00 PM local study reminder alarm after your phone restarts (if reminders are enabled).
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely for Google AdMob ad loading/consent checks when online.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used solely by Google AdMob SDK as declared in Section 3.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, precise GPS location, or broad media storage permissions.

5 Your Privacy Choices & Data Deletion

  • Delete Local Study Data: Open Settings → Reset Study History inside the app to erase quiz attempts and progress anytime, or clear app storage / uninstall the app from Android Settings to delete all local data permanently.
  • Manage Ad Consent & Personalization: Use Settings → Ad Privacy Preferences inside the app (where applicable) or open Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete advertising ID.
  • Turn Off Reminders: Toggle off Daily Evening Test Reminder (5:00 PM) in app Settings or revoke notification permission in Android Settings.
