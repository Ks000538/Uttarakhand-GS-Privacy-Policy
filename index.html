1. No Personal Information or Account Collection

Uttarakhand GS works without user accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera feed, or GPS location.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All app data stays strictly on your Android device using local Room SQLite storage and SharedPreferences, including:

  • Quiz scores, attempt history, and accuracy statistics
  • Bookmarked questions and custom quiz preferences
  • Display theme (Light, Midway, or Dark) and question count settings
  • Optional Test Reminder Notification preferences (enabled/disabled state, your selected reminder time, and your custom reminder message)

None of this local study data is uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

3. No Advertisements & Zero Tracking

Uttarakhand GS is completely ad-free. The app does not display any advertisements and does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking SDKs.

4. Device Permissions

Uttarakhand GS requests only minimal permissions required for core functionality:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, optional on Android 13+): Requested only if you enable the optional Test Reminder Notification in Settings so the app can trigger local study reminders at your chosen time with your custom text.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to re-schedule your local Test Reminder Notification after a device restart if reminders are enabled.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You retain complete control over your data on your device. At any time, you can:

  • Erase all quiz scores and progress inside the app via Settings → Reset Study History
  • Edit or disable your reminder anytime via Settings → Test Reminder Notification
  • Remove all app data permanently by clearing app storage in Android System Settings or uninstalling the app

6. Children's Privacy

Uttarakhand GS is designed for competitive exam aspirants and learners aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age or any other user.
